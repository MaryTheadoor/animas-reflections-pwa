  host (env-driven: `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` switches to the production domain at DNS cutover).

  the customer form is valid. **No real charge has been made yet** — the canary is the one unproven step.

  restore by provenance (`launchHiddenAt`), so a bulk hide is reversible without reviving archived items.

  saved addresses, profile, tracking links.
- **Fulfilment:** Admin → Orders is driven by Firestore (newest 100) with Square as enrichment; each order
  expands to the ship-to address, per-item customisation notes, payment id/status and a **Copy packing
  slip** button. Address line 2 is collected at checkout and forwarded to Square and the mirror.
- **Content:** `/shipping` (rates read from the same config checkout charges) and `/returns` (plain-language
  Terms §7) exist, are linked in the footer and the sitemap, and the FAQ/footer no longer contradict them.
